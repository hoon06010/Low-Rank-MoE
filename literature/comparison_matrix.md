For the main experiment, report total parameters, active parameters, estimated FLOPs, and measured throughput separately. HS↔LL (and the intermediate M point) match total parameters; LS and HL lie below and above that total. Each LR cell matches its corresponding DW cell in total and algebraic active parameters, but the whole 2×2 grid is not isoparametric or iso-FLOP. Same total does not imply same active compute or wall time. The strongest supported distinction is the *separate nonlinear shared-width × native routed SwiGLU rank* interaction with the DW and SHARED_ONLY controls. The earlier 2026 StructMoE PDF reading records a rank/count sweep, ruling out a broad claim that fixed-budget native routed low-rank allocation has never been tested. A StructMoE-style future structural control must fix its exact PDF version and match branch placement, nonlinearity, total parameter and active-compute budgets.
